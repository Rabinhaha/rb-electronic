Battery guide

How to Choose the Right Battery

A few simple checks help you pick a battery that fits your vehicle and works properly.

  1. Check Your Vehicle Model

    The recommended battery depends on your car, motorcycle or scooter model and its manufacturer specifications.

  2. Check Voltage and Capacity

    Check the voltage and the Ah (ampere-hour) capacity your vehicle needs before buying. Your owner's manual or the old battery label usually lists them.

  3. Check Battery Dimensions

    The new battery must fit securely in your vehicle's battery compartment and hold-down bracket.

  4. Check Terminal Position

    The positive (+) and negative (−) terminals must sit on the sides your vehicle's cables are designed for.

  5. Check Starting Power

    The battery must meet your vehicle's starting-current requirement so the engine starts reliably.

  6. Check Battery Technology

    Conventional, maintenance-free and other battery technologies have different requirements. Use the type recommended for your vehicle.

  7. Ask for Assistance

    Not sure? Contact R&B Electronic and we'll help you work out a suitable battery for your vehicle.
